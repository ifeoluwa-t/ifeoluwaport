Turning Currency Friction Into Onboard's Neobank Moment

Owning the launch of EUR and GBP accounts, repositioning Onboard as a true neobank for global professionals.

Year 2026
Category GTM & Product Launches
Skills
Product Marketing Positioning Growth Strategy Creative Direction
Challenge

“Global professionals earning in EUR and GBP faced constant friction — receiving funds in one currency, moving them elsewhere, then finally into Onboard. Every extra step meant lost time, lost money, and lost trust.”

Our Approach

Designing a Direct Path From Earning to Spending

We built EUR and GBP accounts so professionals could finally receive funds in the exact currency they earn, no detours, no extra fees, no waiting.

What I Did

  • Owned the complete product marketing strategy from launch concept through execution, coordinating across product, design, and engineering timelines
  • Crafted positioning that repositioned Onboard from a remittance-first app into a true neobank for global professionals, informing messaging across every channel
  • Directed creative assets for the launch campaign, including in-app announcements, social content, and landing page copy
  • Built comparative analysis against competitor offerings to sharpen our pricing and feature positioning ahead of launch
  • Defined key user segments (freelancers, remote employees, diaspora professionals) to tailor messaging for each audience
  • Designed viral growth loops within the onboarding flow to drive organic account creation beyond paid channels
