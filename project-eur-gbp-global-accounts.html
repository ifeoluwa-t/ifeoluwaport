Onboard to the rescue
Challenge

After we launched our USD accounts, global professionals still asked for EUR and GBP accounts to receive payments. Receiving in one place and sending to another meant lost time, money and trust. They needed to receive EUR & GBP payments into one account and send out from that same account

Our Approach

We gave people a direct path from earning to spending

We built EUR and GBP accounts so professionals could finally receive funds and send money out in the currency they want to.

What I Did

  • Owned the product marketing strategy from launch concept through execution, coordinating across product, design and engineering timelines
  • Directed creative assets for the launch campaign, including in-app announcements, social content, outbound messages and community copy
  • Used comparative analysis against competitor offerings to sharpen our pricing and feature positioning ahead of launch
  • Coordinated with a UK-based marketer to gain entry into United Kingdom and Europe at large
  • Crafted positioning that repositioned Onboard from a remittance-first app into a true neobank for global professionals
