Leading product, content, and brand marketing for Onboard's launch into B2B global payments.

Year 2026
Category Market Entry & Positioning
Skills

Challenge

“Businesses, payment companies, and fintechs needed reliable rails to receive, send, and convert money across borders, but Onboard had never built a product to serve them directly.”

Our Approach

Turning Consumer Trust Into Business Infrastructure

We took everything that made Onboard trusted by individual professionals and rebuilt it as infrastructure businesses could plug into directly.

What I Did

  • Led product marketing strategy for Onboard's first B2B offering, spanning three distinct products: a self-serve Business App, a developer-facing API, and an OTC Desk for high-volume trades
  • Directed content strategy across the launch, translating technical infrastructure (stablecoin liquidity, FX rails, compliance backing) into clear, business-focused messaging
  • Led brand positioning for the pivot, establishing Onboard Business as a regulated, proven company
  • Built messaging around Onboard's existing scale (30,000+ users, $300M+ processed) to give the new business product instant credibility
  • Coordinated launch materials across channels, including the website, sales deck, media assets and partner-facing collateral
  • Aligned product, compliance, and go-to-market teams to ensure consistent positioning across every customer touchpoint
