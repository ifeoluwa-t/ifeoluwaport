Centralising Stablecoin Education for Builders

Co-organising and leading creative direction for a sprint that brought further stablecoin education and adoption to African builders, powered by Base West Africa, cNGN and Uglycash.

Year 2025
Category Campaigns & Activation
Skills
Creative Direction Partnership Management Campaign Strategy Community Engagement
In partnership with

Challenge

“Stablecoin education across Africa was fragmented across different communities. Builders knew stablecoins mattered, but had no source of knowledge from industry leaders to actually learn their value and also see how to use them.”

Our Approach

Bringing Builders, Partners, and Products Into One Room

We centralised stablecoin education into a single, partnership-backed sprint, pairing live sessions with real incentives to participate.

What I Did

  • Co-organised the sprint in partnership with Base West Africa, Uglycash, and cNGN, aligning three organisations around one shared campaign
  • Led creative direction for all sprint materials, including the campaign poster, session promotion, and social content
  • Facilitated partnerships and sponsorships that brought credibility and resources to the sprint
  • Designed and ran a content challenge with a prize pool, incentivising builders to create and share content on stablecoin products
  • Represented Onboard across a live session, bridging practical product use cases with a broader stablecoin view
  • Coordinated 10+ African stablecoin products showcasing their products, giving local builders direct visibility

What We Featured

Everything the Sprint Put on the Table

Six live sessions, one content challenge, and 10+ African products, all in one sprint.
