Test Pilots to the rescue
Challenge

We had just launched Onboard 3.0 a few months back and we were optimising our product to be the best. Every new feature we shipped needed real users to break it, question it, and tell us what wasn't working, before it reached the public. Without that, we were just guessing.

Our Approach

We turned testers into co-builders

We built a community of verified product testers who got early access to new features and gave honest, structured feedback and raw ideas alongside

What I Did

  • Launched and managed Onboard Test Pilots, a private community of verified remote workers and users testing new features before launch
  • Managed the structure for how testers accessed new features, submitted feedback and flagged issues
  • Worked directly with product and engineering teams to translate feedback into actionable fixes and improvements
  • Built ongoing engagement within the community, keeping testers active and invested beyond a new test cycle
  • Recognised and highlighted standout contributors within the community, turning testing into a two-way relationship, not just a feedback group

Results

Smooth user experience and battle-tested features for Onboard users

Before a feature reached the public, it had already been tested, roasted and improved by real users
