Challenge

“African designers had the talent, but not the pathway. Web3 product design roles existed, but almost none of them were opening their doors to designers without a network already inside the industry.”

Our Approach

Building a Pipeline, Not Just a Workshop

We designed a 10-day sprint that paired real mentorship with real products, so participants left with more than a certificate.

What I Did

  • Organised a 2-week design sprint in collaboration with Dacoit Design and other industry brands
  • Built and managed a thriving community of 100+ designers from across Africa
  • Designed a hands-on curriculum pairing participants with industry-leading mentors
  • Coordinated partnerships with design firms and facilitated immersive, real-world design sprints across multiple live products
  • Led creative direction and promotional content for the sprint, driving sign-ups across Africa's design community
  • Directed the mentor and host matching process, ensuring each participant worked alongside experienced product designers
