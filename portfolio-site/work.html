Onchain Design Sprint

Brief

African designers had limited pathways into Web3. We launched a 2-week design sprint to create real opportunities for African talent to break into Web3 product design and earn.

What I Did

  • Built and managed a thriving community of ~50 designers from across Africa
  • Designed a hands-on curriculum and led creative direction across 3 products (Based West Africa, Nedapay, and others)
  • Coordinated partnerships with industry-leading design firms and facilitated immersive design sprints

Results

  • 7+ designers secured paid roles across Web3 products, moving from the sprint directly into employment
  • Created a community of 50 designers with ongoing support and mentorship
  • Established a repeatable, scalable model for skill-based community building

EUR / GBP Global Accounts

Brief

Global professionals earning in EUR and GBP faced friction: receive funds in one currency, move them elsewhere, then to Onboard. We launched EUR & GBP accounts to let users receive in the currency they earn.

What I Did

  • Owned the complete product marketing strategy from launch concept through execution
  • Crafted positioning that positioned Onboard as a true neobank for global professionals
  • Created launch announcements, directed creative assets, and built comparative analysis against competitors
  • Defined key user segments and designed viral loops to drive organic adoption

Results

  • 300+ new accounts created in just one month, proof of real demand
  • $100,000 in deposits within the first month, validating both product-market fit and messaging
  • Shifted Onboard's positioning from remittance app to multi-currency neobank for global builders
  • Unlocked a new revenue stream and user segment
