Giving Onboard's Consumer Experience a Reason to Be Trusted

Leading creative direction and content across a full consumer refresh, from new web pages to a redesigned app and Play Store presence.

Year 2026
Category Branding & Content
Skills

Challenge

“Onboard's consumer experience still looked and read like a crypto app. If professionals were going to trust us with their salaries, every page, every screen, and every store listing needed to earn that trust first.”

Our Approach

Rebuilding Trust, Page by Page

We rebuilt the consumer experience from the ground up, starting with the pages and screens people see before they even sign up.

What I Did

  • Led creative direction and copywriting across the full consumer site refresh, including the Personal Page, Global Accounts page, and Cards page
  • Repositioned messaging on every page to reflect Onboard as a stablecoin neobank for global professionals, moving away from crypto-first language
  • Directed the App Store and Play Store redesign, rewriting screenshot copy and visuals to lead with clarity and outcomes rather than features
  • Wrote and art-directed campaign creative ("Your Money, Minus the Worry") tying the refreshed brand voice across web, app, and social
  • Coordinated with design and product teams to ensure consistency between the new web pages and the in-app experience
  • Used old screens and copy as a baseline to sharpen what needed to change, keeping what already worked and rewriting what didn't
