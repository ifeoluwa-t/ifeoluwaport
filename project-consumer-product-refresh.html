Challenge

Onboard’s consumer experience still looked and read like just a crypto app. If global professionals who earn in USD, EUR and GBP, and also made payments with our Flex Cards, were going to trust us with their transactions, every page, every screen and every store listing needed to be upgraded to show that well

Our Approach

We rebuilt the pages people see before they sign up

We rebuilt the consumer experience, starting with the pages and screens people see before they download Onboard

What I Did

  • Led creative direction and copywriting across the full consumer site refresh, including the Personal Page, Global Accounts page, and Cards page
  • Repositioned messaging on every page to reflect Onboard as a stablecoin neobank for global professionals, moving away from crypto-first language
  • Directed the App Store and Play Store redesign, rewriting screenshot copy and visuals to lead with clarity and outcomes rather than features
  • Coordinated with design and product teams to ensure consistency between the new web pages and the in-app experience
  • Used old screens and copy as a baseline to sharpen what needed to change, keeping what already worked and rewriting what didn't
